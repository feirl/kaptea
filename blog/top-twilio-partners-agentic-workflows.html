The short answer

As of August 2026, Twilio's partner directory lists 14 partners holding the Twilio AI Certification. Of those, only 7 also hold Gold status, Twilio's top consulting tier. Four are Silver, and the rest are Bronze. If your project involves agentic voice, AI agents in customer conversations, or workflow orchestration on Twilio's AI suite, these seven partners are the ones Twilio itself has certified at the highest level on both dimensions: AI capability and consulting delivery.

We should say up front: Kaptea is one of the seven, and we wrote this guide. The methodology is Twilio's own certifications, not our opinion, and the list is presented alphabetically. Every claim here can be checked against Twilio's partner directory.

Why both certifications matter

The Twilio AI Certification (launched March 2025) is earned, not bought. Partners go through deep technical enablement with Twilio's product teams, build real proof-of-concepts on Twilio's AI stack, including ConversationRelay and Conversational Intelligence, and pass a joint evaluation by Twilio's product and partner teams before being approved. In Twilio's words, the programme ensures partners can "lead AI Agent initiatives from day one, bringing validated expertise and deployable solutions."

Gold consulting status is Twilio's highest partner tier, reflecting a sustained track record of certified people, successful deployments and customer evidence. Plenty of partners can talk about AI. The Gold tier is about whether they have repeatedly delivered.

Either certification alone tells you something. Both together answer the question that actually matters when you are buying an implementation: can this partner design an AI agent solution properly, and have they delivered enough real projects to be trusted with yours? Fourteen partners pass the first test. Seven pass both.

How to choose between them

Seven certified partners is still a shortlist, not a decision. The questions that separate them in practice:

  1. Platform or project? Some partners deliver bespoke builds; some bring a product. A bespoke build fits an enterprise with an engineering team and a services budget. A pre-built platform fits an operator who wants the outcome live in days without owning code. Ask each partner which they are, and what happens when you want to change something in a year.
  2. Whose Twilio account does it run on? If the answer is the partner's, your data, your carrier relationships and your negotiated rates are in someone else's hands. Insist on your own account.
  3. What happens after the AI answers? Answering a call is the easy half. Ask how a promise made in that conversation travels across channels to a confirmed resolution, and how you will measure whether it did.
  4. Show me one in production. Not a demo. A reference deployment in your kind of environment, with numbers. Call abandonment, pickup rates, resolution confirmation, whatever the partner claims to improve, ask for the before and after.
  5. Who answers when it breaks at 9pm? Agentic workflows carry live customer conversations. Ask who picks up, and how fast, when something goes wrong on a Friday evening.

What "agentic workflows on Twilio" actually means

Twilio's AI suite provides the building blocks: ConversationRelay for real-time voice AI, Conversational Intelligence for understanding and analysing conversations, and the surrounding products for memory, orchestration and agent handoff. What Twilio deliberately does not prescribe is the implementation: how those blocks become a working journey where AI answers, humans step in at the right moment, context follows the customer across channels, and outcomes get confirmed rather than assumed. That gap between building blocks and a working solution is precisely what this category of partner exists to close, and it is why the AI Certification tests deployable solutions rather than product knowledge.

Frequently asked questions

How many Twilio AI Certified partners are there?
Fourteen worldwide, as of August 2026. The programme launched in March 2025 and admits partners through technical enablement, proof-of-concept builds and joint evaluation with Twilio's product teams.

What does Twilio Gold partner status mean?
Gold is the top tier of Twilio's consulting partner programme, reflecting certified expertise and a sustained record of successful customer deployments. Seven of the fourteen AI Certified partners hold it.

Do I need a partner to use Twilio's AI products?
For simple use cases, not necessarily. For production agentic workflows, where AI holds real customer conversations with escalation, compliance and auditability requirements, an implementation partner typically takes you from months of internal engineering to weeks or, with pre-built platforms, hours.

How do I verify a partner's certifications?
Twilio's partner directory lists every partner with their tier and competencies. Filter by the Twilio AI competency and check the consulting tier on each profile.
