Part 1. Standard Terms of Business

These terms apply to every Kaptea Statement of Work, which also serves as the Order Form referred to below. Where a Statement of Work and these terms conflict, the Statement of Work prevails.

1. What forms the Agreement

1.1 The Agreement consists of the Order Form, these terms, any SOW and the Service Policies identified in the Order Form.

1.2 If terms conflict, the Order Form prevails, then the DPA for data-protection matters, then these terms, then the other Service Policies.

1.3 Purchase-order terms do not apply unless Kaptea expressly agrees in writing.

2. Services and term

2.1 Kaptea will provide the Services stated in the Order Form with reasonable skill and care and materially in accordance with the Documentation.

2.2 Unless the Order Form states otherwise, the initial term is 12 months and renews for 12-month periods unless either Party gives 60 days' notice before renewal.

2.3 Kaptea may improve or change the Services for security, law, provider requirements or operational improvement. It will not materially reduce core paid functionality during a committed term without reasonable notice where practicable.

3. Customer use and responsibilities

3.1 Kaptea grants the Customer a limited, non-exclusive right to use the Services for its business during the term.

3.2 The Customer is responsible for its users, systems, Customer Data, recipients, message content, use cases, consents, opt-outs and legal compliance.

3.3 The Customer shall keep credentials secure, follow Documentation, comply with the Acceptable Use Policy and Third-Party Provider rules, and promptly report suspected misuse or compromise.

3.4 The Customer shall not resell, reverse engineer, interfere with, misuse or use the Services to build a competing service.

4. Communications and third parties

4.1 The Services may rely on Twilio, Telnyx, Meta/WhatsApp, cloud providers, carriers, AI providers and other third parties.

4.2 Communications may fail or be delayed because of carriers, filtering, networks, devices, invalid details, third-party outages or recipient settings. Kaptea does not guarantee delivery to every recipient.

4.3 Provider, carrier, number, registration, regulatory and usage charges may be passed through to the Customer.

4.4 The Services are not emergency dispatch or guaranteed life-safety services unless a separate written addendum says so.

5. Implementation and changes

5.1 The Customer shall provide timely information, access, decisions and cooperation.

5.2 If acceptance tests are stated in an SOW, the Customer shall test within 10 Business Days. A deliverable is accepted when approved, used in production or not validly rejected within that period.

5.3 Work outside scope requires a written change or is charged at Kaptea's then-current rates if authorised by the Customer.

6. Fees and payment

6.1 The Customer shall pay the Fees in the Order Form. Subscriptions are normally invoiced in advance and usage and pass-through charges in arrears.

6.2 Invoices are payable within 30 days. A good-faith dispute must be raised within 15 days and undisputed amounts remain payable.

6.3 Fees exclude VAT. Overdue sums accrue statutory interest and recovery compensation under Irish law.

6.4 Kaptea may increase recurring Fees at renewal on 60 days' notice and may pass through provider or regulatory cost increases on reasonable notice.

6.5 Fees are non-refundable except where these terms expressly say otherwise.

7. Support and security

7.1 The support tier and any availability target are stated in the Order Form and governed by the SLA.

7.2 Kaptea will maintain the security measures in the Information Security Schedule.

7.3 The Customer remains responsible for its devices, identity, CRM, endpoints, networks, permissions, configurations and backups outside the Services.

8. Data protection and data use

8.1 Each Party shall comply with applicable data-protection law. The DPA applies where Kaptea processes personal data for the Customer.

8.2 The Customer retains rights in Customer Data and permits Kaptea and its providers to process it to provide, secure and support the Services.

8.3 Kaptea may use de-identified, aggregated Usage Data for security, billing, analytics and service improvement.

8.4 The Customer shall not submit highly sensitive data unless the Order Form expressly allows it.

8.5 Data export, retention and deletion are governed by the DPA and Exit Schedule.

9. AI Services

9.1 Where AI features are used, the AI Services Schedule applies.

9.2 AI Outputs may be inaccurate or unexpected. The Customer is responsible for review, transparency, lawful use and human oversight.

9.3 The Customer shall not use AI as the sole basis for a decision with legal or similarly significant effects unless expressly agreed and legally compliant.

10. Intellectual property and confidentiality

10.1 Kaptea and its licensors own the Services, platform, software, Documentation, reusable components, methods and improvements. The Customer owns Customer Data and its own materials.

10.2 On full payment, the Customer may use paid-for customer-specific deliverables for its internal business. Reusable Kaptea technology is not assigned.

10.3 Each Party shall protect the other's confidential information, use it only for the Agreement and disclose it only to people who need to know or where law requires.

11. Warranties

11.1 Kaptea warrants reasonable skill and care and material conformity with Documentation.

11.2 Kaptea will use reasonable efforts to correct a verified material breach. If it cannot, the Customer may terminate the affected Service and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused recurring Fees.

11.3 Except as expressly stated, the Services are provided as available and implied warranties are excluded to the extent permitted by law.

12. Liability

12.1 Nothing limits liability that cannot legally be limited, including fraud and death or personal injury caused by negligence.

12.2 Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or loss of profit, revenue, savings, goodwill or business opportunity.

12.3 Each Party's total liability in a 12-month period is limited to the Fees paid or payable for the affected Services in that period.

12.4 The cap is two times that amount for confidentiality, data-protection, security, intellectual-property indemnity and Customer misuse indemnity.

12.5 Kaptea is not liable for a carrier, provider, customer system, internet, destination network or recipient-device failure except to the extent caused by Kaptea's breach.

13. Suspension and termination

13.1 Kaptea may suspend affected Services for non-payment, security risk, unlawful or abusive use, provider direction or material breach, acting immediately where necessary.

13.2 Either Party may terminate for a material breach not remedied within 30 days, or 10 days for non-payment, and immediately for insolvency or serious unlawful use.

13.3 Convenience cancellation during a committed term does not remove the obligation to pay committed Fees.

13.4 On termination, access ends, amounts due remain payable, and the Customer may retrieve data under the Exit Schedule.

14. General

14.1 Neither Party may use the other's name or logo without consent.

14.2 Neither Party is liable for events beyond reasonable control, excluding payment.

14.3 The Agreement may be assigned to an affiliate or business successor with notice; other assignment needs consent.

14.4 Notices shall be sent to the contacts in the Order Form. The Agreement may be signed electronically.

14.5 This Agreement is the entire agreement and changes must be written, except policy updates permitted for law, security or providers. Materially adverse policy changes apply at renewal unless urgently required.

15. Irish law

15.1 Irish law governs the Agreement and the Irish courts have exclusive jurisdiction.

15.2 Before proceedings, the Parties shall try in good faith to resolve disputes through contract-manager and executive escalation.

This Schedule describes the categories of Services Kaptea may supply. Only Services identified in an Order Form are included.

Zoe AI and related services may provide automated or assisted voice, chat, classification, summarisation, routing, transcription, translation or content-generation capabilities.

AI Services remain subject to Schedule 6. The customer is responsible for determining whether a proposed use is lawful, appropriate and subject to human oversight.

Professional Services may include discovery, service design, implementation, configuration, migration, integration, development, testing, training, consulting and optimisation.

The scope, assumptions, dependencies, deliverables, timetable, fees and acceptance criteria shall be stated in an Order Form or Statement of Work.

5. Managed Services

Managed Services may include monitoring, incident coordination, administration, configuration changes, campaign support, reporting, optimisation and liaison with Third-Party Providers.

Managed Services do not transfer the customer's legal responsibility for its communications, recipients, content, consent, records or regulatory obligations.

6. Third-Party Services

The Services may depend on Twilio, SendGrid, Telnyx, Meta/WhatsApp, cloud hosting providers, telecommunications carriers, number registries, artificial intelligence providers, CRM systems and other third parties.

Third-Party Services may be governed by provider terms, acceptable-use rules, country requirements, technical limits and pricing changes. The customer shall comply with all provider terms notified or made available to it.

Kaptea may replace a Third-Party Provider where reasonably necessary, provided that Kaptea does not materially reduce the core functionality purchased without giving reasonable notice where practicable.

Kaptea is not responsible for a Third-Party Provider's act or omission beyond the responsibility expressly assumed in the governing Agreement, but will use commercially reasonable efforts to coordinate resolution where the affected Third-Party Service was procured through Kaptea.

7. Service changes

Kaptea may improve or modify the Services. Kaptea will not materially reduce core paid functionality during a committed subscription term without reasonable notice, except where required for security, law, provider compliance or to address an urgent risk. If a permanent material reduction cannot reasonably be remedied, the parties shall discuss an alternative service or equitable fee adjustment.

Schedule 4. Data Processing Addendum

1. Status and precedence

This Data Processing Addendum (DPA) forms part of the Agreement. It applies where Kaptea processes Customer Personal Data as a processor on behalf of the customer. It prevails over conflicting terms of the Agreement on data-protection matters.

2. Definitions

Controller, processor, data subject, personal data, personal data breach, processing, supervisory authority and special categories of personal data have the meanings given in Applicable Data Protection Law. Customer Personal Data means personal data processed by Kaptea as processor in providing the Services.

3. Roles and instructions

The customer is the controller and Kaptea is the processor of Customer Personal Data, except where the parties are separately independent controllers.

Kaptea shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the customer, including instructions in the Agreement, Order Form, configuration and authorised use of the Services, unless Union or Member State law requires otherwise.

Kaptea shall notify the customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Applicable Data Protection Law and may suspend the affected processing while the parties resolve the issue.

The customer warrants that its instructions are lawful and that it has provided all notices and obtained all consents, lawful bases and permissions required for the processing.

4. Confidentiality and personnel

Kaptea shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are subject to appropriate confidentiality obligations and receive relevant data-protection and security training.

5. Security

Kaptea shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect Customer Personal Data, taking account of the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing and the risk to individuals. The current baseline measures are described in Schedule 5.

6. Subprocessors

The customer grants Kaptea general written authorisation to appoint subprocessors.

Kaptea shall maintain a current subprocessor register identifying the subprocessor, location and purpose.

Kaptea shall give at least 30 days' prior notice of a new subprocessor where reasonably practicable. The customer may object on reasonable data-protection grounds within 15 days.

The parties shall work in good faith to resolve a valid objection. If no reasonable alternative is available, either party may terminate only the affected Service on written notice, with a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused recurring fees.

Kaptea shall impose data-protection obligations on each subprocessor that are no less protective in material respects than this DPA and remains responsible for the subprocessor's performance of those obligations.

7. International transfers

Kaptea shall not transfer Customer Personal Data outside the EEA except in compliance with Applicable Data Protection Law.

Where a transfer is not covered by an adequacy decision, the applicable modules of the European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by Decision (EU) 2021/914 are incorporated by reference, together with the Irish supervisory authority and Irish law selections where permitted.

Kaptea shall implement supplementary measures where reasonably required by a documented transfer assessment.

The customer authorises transfers inherent in the selected communications channels and Third-Party Services, subject to the safeguards described in the subprocessor register.

8. Assistance

Taking account of the nature of processing and information available to Kaptea, Kaptea shall provide reasonable assistance with data-subject requests, security, breach notifications, data-protection impact assessments and prior consultation. Kaptea may charge reasonable fees for assistance that is unusually burdensome or arises from the customer's non-compliance, unless the assistance is required because of Kaptea's breach.

9. Personal data breaches

Kaptea shall notify the customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

The notice shall include information reasonably available about the nature of the breach, categories of data and individuals, likely consequences, measures taken or proposed and a contact point.

Notification is not an admission of fault or liability. The customer is responsible for determining whether to notify a supervisory authority or individuals, unless law assigns that duty to Kaptea.

10. Deletion and return

On termination or the customer's written request, Kaptea shall return or delete Customer Personal Data in accordance with Schedule 7, unless law requires retention. Data may remain in secure backups until overwritten in the ordinary cycle, during which it remains protected and is not actively processed except for recovery, security or legal purposes.

11. Audit and information

Kaptea shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR.

Kaptea may satisfy routine assurance requests using current certifications, audit reports, penetration-test summaries, security questionnaires and policy materials.

Where those materials are insufficient, the customer may conduct one audit per year on at least 30 days' notice, during business hours, subject to confidentiality, security and non-disruption requirements.

On-site audits are permitted only where required by law, a supervisory authority, a material personal data breach attributable to Kaptea, or where documentary assurance is objectively insufficient. The customer bears its audit costs unless the audit identifies a material breach by Kaptea.

emotion recognition in prohibited contexts;
